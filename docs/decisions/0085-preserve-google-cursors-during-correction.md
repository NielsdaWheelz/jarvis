the complete verified `event_cursor` rows in place as the google boundary.
apply the [existing stopped procedure](../runtime-reliability-and-integration.md#e-fresh-history-resets)
to the configured discord channel and the other 17 application tables. this
clears reproduced revisions, their derivatives and genuine intervening central
history, including owner activity, new captures and work. establish fresh native
cuts and preserve original chats, private assets and real google data.

before clearing anything, obtain the genuine canonical pause, settle or
disposition pending work under existing controls, quiesce writers and verify
a full current private recovery snapshot. retain the previous recovery evidence,
including the source identities needed to compare both discarded corpora.
compare the complete cursor rows across the stopped transaction. initialize
paused, verify the fresh cuts and empty history, then obtain ordinary owner
resumption. observe subsequent completed polls and compare provider revision
identities with the stopped inventories. verify that genuine new owner activity
enters the fresh archive. currently due attention remains eligible.

this exception amends adr 0078 only for this operation. it requires no migration
or runtime change. historical revisions stay in the private evidence. normal
google polling uses its existing cursors, admission rules and explicit token-loss
gap. the correction deliberately loses genuine intervening history from the live
corpus. google can still return an earlier revision in a later incremental
response. observed recurrence leaves acceptance open and grants no further wipe.
