the daybook

a proposal for the jarvis conversation, built to be typed in. the left column below is the rail at its true width. everything in it is type or a hairline.

hands, not labels

you write in italic. jarvis answers in roman. the interface murmurs in muted cormorant. with two voices for life, a name on every message says nothing, so the letterform says it.

one sheet

the composer is the unwritten foot of the same page, already in your hand. sending does not restyle your words. the page moves up one line and they are on it.

every motion is a receipt

your words cross the rule when the server holds them and not before. a beat is a confirmed poll. nothing loops and nothing pretends to type.

send

dock the composer

on android today, writing to jarvis replaces the whole screen, so you cannot read him while you answer. this is the largest defect on either client and no amount of typography hides it. the field moves to the foot of the conversation.

one sign, only while it is true

the red pilcrow already means jarvis is waiting on you. it now also stands beside the exact proposal that is owed, and leaves when you have answered. red still means owed and nothing else.

send is always there

on a phone, enter breaks the line, so the word is the only way to send. it is grey with nothing to send and ink when armed, on a full 48dp target.

finish the theme

dividers are material's purple-grey, dialogs are lavender, errors are a second red, and a spinner loops where the presence rules forbid loops. none of it was chosen.

two haptics

one confirm when the server accepts your message. one tick when an awaited reply lands while you are looking. the tap itself is silent, because a tap proves nothing.

the foot of the page

every state the send path already distinguishes keeps a face. an unconfirmed message never enters the record: it stays in the composer, where you can act on it.

underneath

faster, without new machinery

  • your message appears the instant the server accepts it, set from the row the server returns. today it waits two further round trips while the composer sits empty.
  • the tick stays at three seconds. a faster one would blur the beats into a throb to save a second on turns that take many.
  • no streaming transport, for the same reason.

defects found on the way

  • a send whose connection hangs freezes the composer for good. it needs a way out.
  • a file dropped anywhere but the composer replaces the page.
  • the button reads retry send during an ordinary first send.
  • on a phone browser, enter sends and nothing types a new line.
  • the google approval box has no styles at all.

removed from view

  • names and dates on every message. both stay in the markup for screen readers and for copying.
  • the pile of open-this links and every bordered button in the rail.
  • the textarea's resize handle and its two empty rows.
  • android: open exact message and a divider under every message, refresh, new message.
  • the declared body font that was never shipped.
